About

I'm a full-stack developer studying BSc Computer Science with AI Engineering at the University of Birmingham. I build complete software products end to end: React interfaces, REST APIs in FastAPI and Node.js, MongoDB and MySQL databases, authentication and cloud deployment.

My latest app, an astrology platform with a built-in AI agent that answers questions in natural language, is live on the Google Play Store. I've also built business systems for invoicing, stock control and records management, and I'm now focused on AI, machine learning and software engineering.

Before university I ran DC Electricals, my own electrical and CCTV business in Sri Lanka.
