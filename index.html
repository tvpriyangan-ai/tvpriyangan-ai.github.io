About

I'm a Computer Science with AI Engineering student at the University of Birmingham with a strong interest in software engineering, artificial intelligence, electronics, IoT and hardware innovation. I enjoy building complete solutions that combine software and hardware to solve real-world problems.

My interest in technology and innovation started from childhood. I have been involved in research, technical projects, inventions and competitions, developing both practical and creative problem-solving skills. From electronics and embedded systems to web applications, AI platforms and business management systems, I enjoy learning how different technologies can work together.

I have experience working with Python, JavaScript, React, FastAPI, Node.js, databases, REST APIs, AI agents, Arduino, electronics and cloud deployment. I particularly enjoy turning an idea into a working product — from designing the system and developing the software to building and testing the hardware.

Alongside technology, I have developed strong leadership, business management, communication and practical problem-solving skills through my professional and project experience.

Before starting university, I founded and ran my own electrical and CCTV business, DC Electricals, in Sri Lanka, gaining hands-on experience in business operations, electrical installations, electronics, CCTV systems, project management and customer solutions.

My long-term goal is to work at the intersection of AI, software, robotics and intelligent hardware, developing innovative technologies that can solve real-world problems.
