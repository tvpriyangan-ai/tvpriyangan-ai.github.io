About

A passion for technology, innovation and hands-on experimentation has been part of my journey since childhood, with a particular fascination for robotics technology.

Currently pursuing a BSc in Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence at Birmingham City University, I continuously expand my knowledge through curiosity-driven learning, online courses and practical projects.

Alongside my university studies, I actively explore software development, programming languages, full-stack development, cloud computing with AWS, AI and electronics. I enjoy learning new technologies independently and applying my knowledge to build practical solutions that combine software, intelligent hardware and real-world applications.

As the Director of DC Electricals in Sri Lanka, I have also gained valuable experience in electrical installations, CCTV systems, business management and problem-solving. My long-term ambition is to become a Robotics Research Scientist, advancing AI and robotics through research, experimentation and innovative technologies that address real-world challenges.
