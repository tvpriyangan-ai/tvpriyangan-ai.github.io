About

I'm a Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence student at Birmingham City University with a strong passion for AI, software engineering, hardware, electronics, robotics and innovation. My interest in technology started from childhood, where I explored ideas through research, technical projects, inventions, practical experiments and competitions. I enjoy turning ideas into working solutions by combining software, AI, electronics and intelligent hardware to solve real-world problems.

I have hands-on experience with Python, JavaScript, Java, React, FastAPI, Node.js, databases, REST APIs, AI agents, Arduino and electronics, and I enjoy developing complete systems from concept and interface design to backend development, hardware integration and deployment. Alongside technology, I have developed strong leadership, business management, communication and practical problem-solving skills.

Before starting university, I founded and ran my own electrical and CCTV business, DC Electricals, in Sri Lanka, gaining valuable hands-on experience in electrical installations, CCTV systems, electronics, project management and customer solutions. My long-term goal is to work at the intersection of AI, software, robotics, invention and intelligent hardware, creating innovative technologies that make a meaningful impact on real-world problems.
